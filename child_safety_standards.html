Church On App - Child Safety and CSAE Standards

Last Updated: July 2026

Developer: Godfrey Kalambo

Church On App has a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to maintaining a safe, secure community environment.

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users are strictly prohibited from uploading, sharing, transmitting, or streaming any visual depictions of minors engaging in sexually explicit conduct, grooming behaviors, or any form of exploitation.

Zero Tolerance: Any content depicting or promoting the sexual exploitation of minors will result in immediate, permanent account termination and reporting to law enforcement authorities.

2. Enforcement and Content Monitoring

We actively review flagged content within our system. Our enforcement measures include:

  • Automated content scanning for known CSAM signatures
  • Manual review of all flagged reports within 24 hours
  • Immediate, permanent account termination for confirmed violations
  • Preservation of evidence for law enforcement purposes

3. Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report child safety concerns directly in-app via our built-in reporting features. These include:

  • Report button on all user profiles
  • Report option on all shared content (posts, messages, media)
  • Direct contact with our safety team via email

4. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Confirmed violations of child safety laws will be immediately reported to regional and national law enforcement authorities, as well as global clearinghouses including:

  • National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC)
  • Internet Watch Foundation (IWF)
  • Local and national law enforcement agencies as required by law

5. Age Restrictions

Church On App is not intended for users under the age of 13. Users between the ages of 13 and 18 must have parental or guardian consent to use the platform.
