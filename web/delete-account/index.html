Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to hello@churchonapp.com from the email address registered on your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your full name and registered email address in the body of the email.
  4. We will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation once complete.

What Data Is Deleted

The following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number, photo)
  • Your account settings and preferences
  • Your activity history and session data

What Data Is Retained

The following data may be retained for legal and regulatory compliance:

  • KYC documents — retained for 5 years after account closure (regulatory requirement)
  • Transaction records — retained for 7 years (tax and audit compliance)

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@churchonapp.com.
