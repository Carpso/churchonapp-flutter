# Influencer & Church Partnership Strategy

---

## Target Influencer Profiles

| Tier | Followers | Type | Examples | Approach |
|------|-----------|------|----------|----------|
| **Gold** | 50k+ | Prominent pastors, gospel musicians, Christian celebrities | Pastor D, Gospel artist | Formal partnership with compensation |
| **Silver** | 10-50k | Growing pastors, worship leaders, Christian content creators | Regional pastors, YouTube preachers | Product + commission |
| **Bronze** | 1-10k | Youth leaders, church admins, Bible teachers | Active church members | Free trial + referral rewards |

## Outreach Templates

### Cold DM / Email — Gold Influencer

```
Subject: Partnership Opportunity — Church On App × [Name]

Dear [Name],

I'm reaching out because your ministry has a significant impact on 
the Zambian Christian community, and I believe there's a powerful 
synergy between what you do and Church On App.

Church On App is Zambia's leading church management platform, used by 
churches across the country. We're looking for trusted voices like 
yours to help us reach more pastors and church members.

We'd love to explore a partnership that includes:

1. Sponsored content featuring your ministry on our platform
2. Cross-promotion to our combined audiences
3. Potential revenue share for church referrals

Would you be open to a brief call to discuss?

Blessings,
[Name]
Church On App
```

### Cold DM — Silver Influencer

```
Hello [Name]! We love your content and how you're serving the 
Christian community in Zambia. 

We'd like to partner with you to help promote Church On App — a 
platform that helps churches go digital with giving, streaming, 
Bible study, and more.

We offer:
• Free access to all premium features for your church

Interested in learning more? 🙏
```

### Cold DM — Bronze Influencer / Church Member

```
Hi [Name]! We see you're active in your church community. 

Church On App is looking for ambassadors to help spread the word. 
If your church isn't on the app yet, we'd love to onboard them 
for free.

As an ambassador, you get:
• Free premium features
• Referral rewards
• Early access to new features

Reply "YES" if you're interested!
```

## Partnership Tiers

### Bronze Tier — "Church Ambassador"
| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Free premium access | ✗ |
| Referral commission | 5% of onboarding fee per referred church |
| Featured content | ✗ |
| Co-branded materials | ✗ |
| **Requirement** | Share app with 3+ churches, post 2x/month |

### Silver Tier — "Digital Missionary"
| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Free premium access | ✓ (6 months) |
| Referral commission | 10% of onboarding fee per referred church |
| Featured content | Quarterly feature on our channels |
| Co-branded materials | Social media kit |
| **Requirement** | Create 4 pieces of content/month, attend partner calls |

### Gold Tier — "Kingdom Partner"
| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Free premium access | ✓ (Lifetime) |
| Referral commission | 15% of onboarding fee per referred church |
| Featured content | Monthly feature + dedicated campaign |
| Co-branded materials | Full media kit + custom content |
| Monthly retainer | Negotiable |
| **Requirement** | 8+ pieces of content/month, exclusive category rights, quarterly strategy review |

## Referral Structure for Church Signups

| Action | Commission (Bronze) | Commission (Silver) | Commission (Gold) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Church signs up (pastor) | K75 on K1,500 fee | K150 | K225 |
| Church signs up (bishop) | K100 on K2,000 fee | K200 | K300 |
| Church upgrades to paid | 5% of annual fee | 10% | 15% |
| Referred pastor joins congregation | — | K50 flat | K100 flat |

## Tracking & Measurement

1. **Unique referral links** — Each partner gets a custom link (churchonapp.com/ref/[name])
2. **Referral codes** — Partners get a code for pastors to enter during registration
3. **Dashboard** — Partners can see their referrals and commissions in the app
4. **Monthly payout** — Commissions paid via mobile money on the 15th of each month
5. **Quarterly review** — Performance review, tier adjustments, strategy alignment

---

## Partnership Lifecycle

**Month 1:** Onboarding — welcome kit, training, first content guidelines  
**Month 2:** Activation — first content published, referral link active  
**Month 3:** Growth — review performance, optimize messaging  
**Month 4+:** Retention — quarterly bonuses, tier upgrades, special campaigns
