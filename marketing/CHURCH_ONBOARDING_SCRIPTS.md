# Church Onboarding Scripts — COA Employee Sales Toolkit

---

## Phone Call Scripts

### Call 1: Cold Call — Intro & Value Prop

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Objective:** Get permission to send more info / book a demo

```
Agent: "Hello, am I speaking with Pastor [Name]?"

Pastor: "Yes, speaking."

Agent: "Good morning Pastor. My name is [Name] from Church On App. 
I'm calling because we've been helping churches in Zambia go digital 
— and I wanted to see if you have five minutes to hear how your 
ministry could benefit."

[Pause — let them respond]

Agent: "Great. Simply put, Church On App is a mobile platform that 
gives your members digital giving with mobile money, live streaming 
of your services, Bible study tools, event management, and even 
ride-sharing — all in one app. Over [X] churches in Zambia already 
use it. The best part? You get a 30-day free trial. No commitment. 
Would you be open to a quick 20-minute demo this week?"

[Handle objection or confirm]

Agent: "Perfect. I'll send you a WhatsApp with more info and we can 
set up a time. What's your preferred day — Wednesday or Thursday?"
```

---

### Call 2: Follow-up Call (After Sending Info)

**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Objective:** Book a demo or close

```
Agent: "Hello Pastor [Name], it's [Name] from Church On App. I sent 
you some info on WhatsApp last week — did you get a chance to look?"

[Pause]

Agent: "Great. As you saw, the platform handles giving, streaming, 
Bible study, and administration. I'd love to show you how it works 
in a 20-minute screen share. You'll see exactly how your members 
will use it and how you can manage everything from your phone."

[Handle objection]

Agent: "I understand. Let me ask — what's the biggest challenge your 
church faces with digital or administration right now?"

[Listen, then address]

Agent: "That's exactly what Church On App solves. Let me show you. 
How about [day] at [time]?"
```

---

### Call 3: Closing Call (After Demo)

**Duration:** 5-7 minutes  
**Objective:** Get sign-up commitment

```
Agent: "Pastor [Name], thanks again for your time on the demo. Based 
on what we covered, I think Church On App would be a great fit for 
[Church Name]. The 30-day free trial means there's zero risk — we set 
everything up, onboard your members, and if it's not working after 30 
days, no charge at all."

Agent: "Here's what I need from you: just confirm the church name, 
your preferred contact number for the treasurer, and I'll handle 
everything else. Your members will be using it within 24 hours."

[Pause]

Agent: "Shall we proceed? I'll send you the onboarding link right 
now on WhatsApp."
```

## In-Person Pitch Script

**Setting:** Church office or after service  
**Duration:** 5-7 minutes  
**Props:** Phone with app open

```
"Pastor, thank you for your time. I want to show you something that's 
helping churches across Zambia."

[Open app on phone]

"This is Church On App. In one app, your members can:
- Give offerings using MTN or Airtel money — no cash needed
- Watch your sermons live or recorded
- Read the Bible with reading plans
- Request prayer and share testimonies
- See church events and RSVP
- Even book Carpso rides for transport

And for you, Pastor — you get a dashboard where you can:
- See exactly who's giving and how much
- Track member attendance and engagement
- Broadcast announcements to everyone
- Generate service reports

The best part? Your church gets 30 days free. I'll set it up for you 
right now. All I need is your church name and your treasurer's phone 
number. Let's do it."

[Open phone, start registration process]
```

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already use WhatsApp" | "WhatsApp is great for chat, but it can't handle giving, live streaming with recording, Bible study plans, or member management. Church On App does all of that in one place — members don't need to jump between apps." |
| "We already use YouTube" | "YouTube is good for reach, but Church On App gives you giving integrated with your streams, member engagement tracking, and a private community for your church — not just a public broadcast." |
| "We don't have budget" | "It's completely free for 30 days. After that, the fee is minimal compared to what you'll gain in giving and engagement. Most churches see a 30-50% increase in giving that more than covers the cost." |
| "Our congregation is small" | "Church On App works great for small churches! In fact, smaller churches often see the biggest impact because it gives your members tools they wouldn't have otherwise. And the 30-day free trial means zero risk." |
| "Our members aren't tech-savvy" | "The app is designed to be simple. Give with mobile money — everyone knows how to do that. Watch a video — just tap play. And we provide onboarding support to help your members get started." |
| "We already have a website" | "A website is important, but most of your members interact on their phones. Church On App is a mobile-first platform with push notifications, mobile money integration, and features your website can't offer." |
