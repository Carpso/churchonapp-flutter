## 2. Tutorial Walkthrough (3 minutes)

**Tone:** Helpful, instructional, patient  
**Music suggestion:** Soft background pad, low volume

```
[SCENE 1: Getting Started — 0:00 to 0:30]
Welcome to Church On App. Let's get you started.

First, download the app from the Play Store or App Store. Open it 
and you'll see the welcome screen. Tap "Get Started".

Create your account with your email address or phone number. You'll 
receive a verification code — enter it to verify your account.

Once verified, you can either join an existing church or create your 
own. If your church is already on the app, search for it by name. 
If not, you can start a new church registration.

[SCENE 2: Your Profile — 0:30 to 1:00]
After joining a church, tap the profile icon to set up your profile. 
Add your photo, full name, and phone number.

Your profile is how other church members recognize you on the 
prayer wall, in Bible Quiz, and in community features. Make sure 
your details are accurate, especially your phone number — this is 
how you'll receive giving confirmations.

[SCENE 3: Digital Giving — 1:00 to 1:30]
To give, tap the "Give" button on the home screen. Select your 
giving type — Tithe, Offering, Building Fund, or Missions.

Enter the amount in Zambian Kwacha. Choose your mobile money 
network — MTN, Airtel, or Zamtel. You'll receive a prompt on your 
phone to confirm the payment with your mobile money PIN.

That's it. Your giving is processed instantly and your church is 
notified. You'll receive a confirmation in the app.

[SCENE 4: Bible & Reading Plans — 1:30 to 2:00]
Tap "Bible" to access the full scripture. You can read by book, 
chapter, and verse. Tap any verse to highlight it, bookmark it, 
or add a note.

For daily study, tap "Reading Plans". Choose from devotional plans, 
book studies, or topical plans. The app tracks your progress and 
sends reminders so you never miss a day.

The "Verse of the Day" appears on your home screen — tap it to 
read, share, or save.

[SCENE 5: Community Features — 2:00 to 2:30]
The "Prayer Wall" lets you share prayer requests with your church 
community. Tap the plus button, type your request, and post. Other 
members can pray and comment.

Want to share a testimony or update? Use the "Feed" to post updates, 
photos, and encouragement for your church family.

Challenge others in the "Bible Quiz Arena" — answer questions on 
scripture, earn points, and climb the leaderboard.

[SCENE 6: Events & More — 2:30 to 3:00]
Check the "Events" tab to see upcoming church activities. Tap any 
event to RSVP, see details, and add it to your calendar.

For transport, use "Carpso Ride" to book a ride or request a 
delivery. Track your driver in real-time.

That covers the basics! Explore the app and discover all the 
features. For more help, tap "Support" in your profile settings 
or contact our team directly.
```

## 4. Church Demo Presentation (5 minutes)

**Tone:** Professional, confident, persuasive  
**Music suggestion:** Inspirational orchestral, starts subtle, builds

```
[INTRODUCTION — 0:00 to 0:30]
Good morning. Thank you for having me. Today I want to show you a 
platform that's transforming how Zambian churches operate and engage 
their members.

Church On App is Zambia's first all-in-one church management and 
community platform. It's used by churches from Lusaka to Ndola, 
from Kitwe to Livingstone. And I believe it can transform your 
ministry too.

[THE PROBLEM — 0:30 to 1:00]
Let's start with the challenges we've heard from pastors like you. 

Members forget cash for offerings. Young people are disengaged 
during the week. You're juggling multiple platforms — WhatsApp for 
messages, YouTube for streaming, spreadsheets for member data. 
It's exhausting.

And you're missing opportunities — members in diaspora who want to 
give and participate, visitors who want to connect, youth who need 
engagement beyond Sunday.

[THE SOLUTION — 1:00 to 2:00]
Church On App solves all of this in one platform.

Let me show you the member experience. *[Switch to demo]* 

A member opens the app and sees the Bible verse of the day. They 
can read their reading plan, give their offering with mobile money 
in 10 seconds, watch last Sunday's sermon, post a prayer request, 
and RSVP for the upcoming event — all without leaving the app.

*[Switch back]*

For you as the pastor, the admin dashboard shows who's giving, 
which members are engaged, what events are coming up, and how many 
people watched the live stream. All in real time.

[FEATURES DEEP DIVE — 2:00 to 3:30]
Let me highlight the key features:

*Digital Giving:* Your members give with MTN, Airtel, or Zamtel 
mobile money. No cash. No envelopes. No counting. You see every 
transaction immediately. Pastors report 30 to 50 percent increases 
in giving within the first month.

*Live Streaming & Sermon Vault:* Stream services live to your entire 
congregation — including members abroad. Sermons are automatically 
recorded and stored so members can watch anytime.

*Bible Study:* Reading plans, verse of the day, book studies, 
topical studies. Members stay engaged with the Word every day.

*Community:* Prayer wall, testimonies, and feeds keep your church 
connected throughout the week. Youth engagement is transformed by 
the Bible Quiz Arena.

*Carpso Ride:* Church transport and delivery services. Members can 
request rides and track drivers in real time.

*Events:* Create, promote, and manage events with RSVPs and ticket 
sales.

[PRICING & NEXT STEPS — 3:30 to 4:30]
Here's the best part. Your church gets a 30-day free trial. No 
credit card. No commitment. We set everything up for you.

After the trial, the onboarding fee is K1,500 for a pastor-led 
church or K2,000 for a bishop-led church. That covers your church 
for one year of full access to all features.

We provide onboarding support to help your members get started. 
We'll send you materials, guides, and even do a training session 
with your tech team.

[CLOSE — 4:30 to 5:00]
Churches across Zambia are already seeing the impact. More giving. 
More engagement. More community. 

I invite you to join them. Start your free trial today. Onboard 
your church at churchonapp.com, or give me your card and I'll set 
it up for you right now.

Let's take your ministry further. Thank you.
```
