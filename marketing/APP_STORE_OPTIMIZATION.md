## Review Solicitation Strategy

### In-App Prompt (After Positive Action)
Trigger after user:
- Successfully gives offering → 3 seconds later
- Completes Bible reading plan → next app open
- Wins a Bible Quiz → immediately
- Watches a full live stream → after stream ends

**Prompt text:**
"Enjoying Church On App? Tap ⭐ to rate us and help other churches discover the app!"

### WhatsApp / SMS Follow-up
Sent 7 days after user first gives digitally:
```
🙏 Thanks for using Church On App! 

Your support helps us reach more churches in Zambia. 
Could you spare 30 seconds to leave a review?

👉 [Review link]

Bless you! 🙏
```

### Pastors' Email
Sent 14 days after church onboarding:
```
Subject: Help us serve more churches like yours

Pastor [Name],

We're so glad [Church Name] is part of the Church On App family.

Would you mind leaving a review on Google Play? Your feedback helps 
other pastors discover the platform and helps us improve.

👉 [Review Link]

Thank you for being part of this journey.

[Name]
Church On App Team
```
