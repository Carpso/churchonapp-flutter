## Engagement Tactics

1. **Reply to every comment** within 2 hours (posting time)
2. **Story polls** — "Does your church use digital giving?" Yes/No
3. **Quiz questions** — "What's your favorite Bible book?" in comments
4. **User-generated content** — Repost churches using the app
5. **Church shoutouts** — Feature one church per week
6. **Influencer takeovers** — Let a pastor run the account for a day
7. **Giveaway** — "Tag your church for a chance to win free onboarding"
