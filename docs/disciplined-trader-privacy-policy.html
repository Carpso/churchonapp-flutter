Privacy Policy

Disciplined Trader

Effective Date: July 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Disciplined Trader ("we", "our", or "the App") is a financial utility tool designed to help forex traders manage risk, track growth, and build discipline. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

Disciplined Trader is an offline-first application. Most of your data never leaves your device.

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Account balance and risk preferences — used for lot size calculations
  • Growth planner projections — your compounding targets
  • Daily journal entries — your checklist completions and streak data
  • App settings — currency preferences, onboarding state

This data is stored in your device's local storage (SharedPreferences) and is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Data Sent to Third-Party Services

  • Frankfurter API — We fetch live exchange rates from frankfurter.app to display accurate currency conversions. This request includes your selected currency pair (e.g., USD/ZAR) but no personal information.
  • ForexFactory Calendar — We fetch economic calendar events from faireconomy.media. No personal data is sent.

2.3 Analytics (Future)

If analytics are enabled in future versions, we will use Supabase to collect anonymous usage data (screen views, feature usage) to improve the app. No personal or financial data will be collected.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Lot size calculations — Your balance and risk settings are used solely to compute position sizes on your device.
  • Growth tracking — Your projections are displayed to you only.
  • Journal streaks — Your discipline checklist is stored locally to track your consistency.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. We do not run advertisements. We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII).

5. Third-Party Links

The App contains links to Exness (a forex broker) for user convenience. Clicking these links opens an external browser. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Exness or any other third-party website. Please review their privacy policies separately.

6. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your financial data to any server. Exchange rate requests are made over HTTPS and contain no personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the App and on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear all local data by uninstalling the App or clearing app data in your device settings.
  • Request information about any data we may collect in the future.
