# Church On App - App Store Listing

## App Name
Church On App

## Short Description (30 chars)
Church community platform

## Long Description
Church On App is the all-in-one digital platform for churches across Africa. Built for the African church experience, it combines giving, community, Bible study, and ministry tools in one powerful app.

### Features

**GIVING & FINANCES**
- Secure mobile money giving (MoMo) via Lipila
- Digital tithing with instant receipts
- Church coins rewards system
- Multi-currency wallet
- Giving history and statements

**COMMUNITY**
- Live prayer requests and testimonies
- Church chat and direct messaging
- Community groups and fellowships
- Polls and announcements
- Event management and ticketing

**BIBLE STUDY**
- Full Bible with multiple versions
- Daily devotions and reading plans
- Bible quizzes and challenges
- AI-powered sermon notes and study prompts
- Audio Bible

**MINISTRY TOOLS**
- Live streaming (HLS adaptive quality)
- Church website builder
- Volunteer scheduling
- CRM and donor management
- Role-based admin dashboard

**CONNECT**
- Audio and video calls
- Inter-church network
- Pastor's corner
- Church discovery

**PERFORMANCE**
- Works offline with smart caching
- Adaptive streaming for slow connections
- Optimized for low-end Android devices
- Minimal data usage

### For Churches
Church On App gives your church a complete digital presence:
- Manage members and roles
- Collect tithes and offerings digitally
- Stream services live
- Build your church website
- Schedule volunteers
- Track donor giving

### For Members
Stay connected with your church community:
- Give securely from your phone
- Join live prayer sessions
- Watch sermons live or on-demand
- Read the Bible daily
- Connect with your pastor and community

### Built for Africa
- Mobile money integration (MoMo, Airtel Money)
- Works on 2G/3G connections
- Offline-first design
- Low data usage
- Local language support coming soon
